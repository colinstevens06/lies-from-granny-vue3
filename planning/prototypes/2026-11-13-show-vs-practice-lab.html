What the four personas said

Where they agreed

  • No second row per day. All three who split the answer chose one extra "show" value on the same day, empty unless it differs. Nobody proposed asking 180 questions.
  • Show answers only matter on Friday and Saturday.
  • Seed Anna with a rule, not a list. "The night before a No Sunday is no-show" derives her Saturdays from Sundays already listed.
  • Two possible-date lists, one for practices and one for show nights.

Where they split

  • Interaction designer: corner tag on Fri/Sat Yes cells (option C). Admits the tag is a small target next to the main one.
  • Anna and Fives: an extra tap state (option B), plus a "check your show nights" step before sign-off. Warns that Fives will stop on the new state by accident.
  • Scheduler and data: same storage, with a Practice / Show switch on the calendar. Warns it is a whole mode built for one member.
  • Premise-challenger: don't store show answers for every day. The grid already is a practice grid; ask about show nights only when a venue offers them (option E).

Checked against the repo

  • True: the app only ever locks in practices. Shows are hand-added to shows.ts, so today's yellow "possible" is a practice answer that reads like a show answer.
  • Corrected: one report said Anna would audit 13 Saturdays in this round. The round has 13 Saturdays; 7 are before a working Sunday.
  • Unverified: long-press was offered as an accelerator. It isn't prototyped here: on iOS it competes with text selection and nobody finds it.
  • The sharpest point: when the show answer silently follows the day answer, every mistake lands on "yes, she can play". That is why the sign-off check is a toggle.

Reading this page

Real

The week grid's sizes (54px label, seven cells, 4px gaps, 46px tall), the tap cycle, the answer colors, the calendar's four day states and five dots, and Anna's seed rule (every Fri and Sat, long-weekend Sundays every 14 days from Jan 10, 2027). The readout numbers are counted from the page as you tap.

Invented

Everyone else's answers, the booked practice on Jan 23, and the venue offer of Feb 13 and Feb 20. Fives starts blank for Jan 1 to 16 so you can feel entering from nothing. "Fill a weekday at once" and Copy list are left out. Sign off never blocks here.
