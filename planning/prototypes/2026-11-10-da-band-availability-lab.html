Da Band availability lab

One form going out to the band, "March show planning", with two parts: availability and a yes/no question. Plus the shared calendar, "Edit mine" and the admin screen, at real width in a 720px-tall phone frame. Version 9, Nov 10: this is the reference for the build. Use "Your form" to walk the whole life of a form, from Fresh to Dates locked. Everything in the frame is clickable, and your answers carry across screens.

What's real and what's invented

  • Real: the roster, the 720px page width, the site colors and fonts, Dec 6 (tentative, Burtonsville), the Jan 1–Mar 31 range, and Anna's seed (every Fri and Sat, her long-weekend Sundays).
  • Invented: everyone else's answers, and Anna's weekday answers. Franny has answered only through Feb 14, so March shows what an unfinished round looks like.
  • Measured live: every number in the readout comes from the rendered frame.

How faithful it is

The Next practice card and the page width copy the built /da-band page. The site nav is a stand-in. The frame is plain HTML, not PrimeVue, so judge structure and flow here and polish in the real app.

  • Sign in as Nick and open Admin to see "Access denied".
